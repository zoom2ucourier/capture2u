# Capture2u: getting started

Capture2u replaces Loom from **Thursday 3 December**. It does the same job: record your screen, get a link, share it.

## 1. Sign in (1 minute)

1. Go to **https://capture2u.locate2u.com**
2. Choose **Sign in with Google** and pick your work account.

You should land in the company workspace. If you see an empty workspace of your own instead, ask in the team channel and you will be added.

## 2. Record

There are two ways. Both work in Chrome.

**Quickest: from the site**
1. Open Capture2u and choose **Record a Cap** in the left menu.
2. Choose your whole screen, one window, or one tab. Turn the camera and microphone on or off.
3. Press stop when you are done. The link is ready straight away.

**Handiest: the Chrome extension**
1. Install the **Cap** extension from the Chrome Web Store.
2. Right-click the extension icon and open **Options**.
3. In **Cap URL** enter `https://capture2u.locate2u.com` and save.
4. Sign in when asked. From then on, click the icon on any page to record.

The extension also lets you draw on the page and blur things you don't want shown.

## 3. Share

- Copy the link and paste it anywhere. **Anyone with the link can watch**, the same as Loom.
- In Slack, the video plays right in the message.
- To lock a video, open its menu (the three dots) and add a password, or change who it is shared with.
- People outside the company can watch but cannot comment.

Each video gets a transcript, a title, a summary and chapters automatically a few minutes after you stop recording.

## 4. Videos are deleted after one year

To keep storage costs down, **every video is deleted automatically one year after it was recorded.**

If a video needs to stay (training, how-to guides, anything people will keep coming back to):

1. Find it in your library.
2. Open its menu (the three dots).
3. Choose **Keep (don't auto-delete)**.

You can do this at any time before the year is up.

## 5. Your old Loom videos

- Old Loom videos are **not** being moved. They stay in Loom, and links you have already shared should keep working.
- After our Loom subscription ends you will **not be able to download** from Loom.
- **If there is an old Loom video you must keep, download it from Loom before the subscription ends**, then upload it to Capture2u (**Import Media** in the left menu) and mark it Keep.
