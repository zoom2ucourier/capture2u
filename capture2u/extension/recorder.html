Record your screen

Pick what to share, press Start, and get a link when you stop.

Chrome's own pages can't be recorded from, so the recorder opened here.

Capture2u is installed. Sign in once with your work email and you're ready to record.

  • Click the Capture2u icon in the toolbar on any page to open the recorder there.
  • Press Alt+Shift+S to start recording with your last settings, and again to stop.
  • While recording, open the round badge to pause, draw on the page, blur something private or turn click highlights off.
